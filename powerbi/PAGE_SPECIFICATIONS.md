# Power BI Page Specifications

## 1 — Enterprise Command Center
Question: Where is MEDNEXUS losing operational value?
- KPI cards: Revenue, Operating Cost, OEE, OLI, MORI, On-Time Delivery
- line chart: Revenue vs Operating Cost by month
- bar chart: top value-loss drivers
- risk trend: MORI by month
- action table: DecisionQueue
- slicers: month, plant (when detailed tables are connected)

## 2 — Finance & Business Health
- monthly revenue/cost/margin trend
- budget variance
- scrap/downtime/logistics/technology cost breakdown
- commentary text explaining illustrative cost assumptions

## 3 — People & Recruitment
- required vs actual headcount
- vacancy trend
- overtime and absence
- recruitment funnel and time-to-fill

## 4 — Manufacturing & Quality
- OEE component trend
- OLI trend
- throughput and good units
- defect Pareto
- product/line drill-through

## 5 — Equipment & Reliability
- downtime by machine
- maintenance type
- predictive risk distribution
- false-negative/false-positive model note

## 6 — Supply Chain & Logistics
- supplier reliability
- shortage hours
- on-time delivery
- delay distribution

## 7 — Healthcare Customer Service
- customer order volume
- delivery delay
- service tickets by issue type
- no patient-identifiable data

## 8 — Technology & Data Trust
- incident duration/severity
- adoption
- data-trust score and quality status

## 9 — Prediction / Forecast / Risk
- model metrics
- failure-risk trend
- demand forecast
- MORI component trend

## 10 — Scenario & Decision Intelligence
- scenario selector
- simulated opportunity value
- good-units and downtime comparison
- DecisionQueue table
- explicit "Simulated" badge/label on scenario visuals
