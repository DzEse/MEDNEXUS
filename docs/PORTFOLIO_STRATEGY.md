# Portfolio Presentation Strategy

## Recommended description
MEDNEXUS is a simulated enterprise analytics engagement designed to demonstrate how financial, workforce, manufacturing, quality, supply-chain, logistics, healthcare-service and technology data can be integrated into an evidence-backed decision system.

## Personal positioning
My background combines mathematics, engineering, manufacturing, medical-technology operations and analytics training. MEDNEXUS brings those disciplines together by moving beyond isolated reporting toward understanding how operational decisions relate to enterprise performance.

## Evidence to demonstrate
Show the repository architecture, lineage, SQL, tests, model evaluation, scenario transparency and Power BI decision story. Do not present MEDNEXUS as real employment.
