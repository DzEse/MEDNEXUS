# Project Charter

## Project
MEDNEXUS — Enterprise Operational Intelligence & Decision Analytics Platform.

## Engagement type
Fictional enterprise; simulated analytics engagement.

## Executive question
Where is MEDNEXUS losing operational value, why is it happening, what is likely to happen next, and which intervention should management prioritize?

## Objectives
1. Integrate interconnected business domains into one traceable analytical model.
2. Quantify operational loss, quality loss, risk and service outcomes.
3. Apply descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, forecast, scenario and decision analytics only where methodologically appropriate.
4. Produce Power BI-ready outputs and a documented semantic model.
5. Demonstrate reproducibility, validation, governance and transparent assumptions.

## In scope
Finance, workforce, recruitment, manufacturing, quality, maintenance/reliability, supply chain, logistics, healthcare-customer service, technology operations, risk, forecasting, scenarios, decision queue, Power BI design.

## Out of scope
Real patient-identifiable information, real MEDNEXUS employment claims, unverified causal claims, fabricated business savings, production deployment, real-time streaming, 3D factory twin, and a prebuilt PBIX file.
