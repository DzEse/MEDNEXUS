# Business Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Decision enabled |
|---|---|---|
| BR-01 | Reconcile revenue, cost and margin proxies monthly | Identify financial pressure |
| BR-02 | Compare required vs available workforce capacity | Prioritize hiring/capacity actions |
| BR-03 | Measure production attainment, OEE and OLI | Identify productive-capacity loss |
| BR-04 | Quantify defects, FPY proxy, scrap and Pareto concentration | Focus quality investigation |
| BR-05 | Measure downtime, maintenance and machine risk | Prioritize reliability intervention |
| BR-06 | Quantify supplier reliability and shortage exposure | Prioritize supply risk |
| BR-07 | Measure on-time delivery and customer-service exceptions | Improve service reliability |
| BR-08 | Measure technology incidents and downtime | Assess data/system decision risk |
| BR-09 | Forecast near-term demand with baseline validation | Plan capacity |
| BR-10 | Compare intervention scenarios transparently | Support management choice |
| BR-11 | Produce a prioritized decision queue with confidence and limitations | Convert evidence into action |
